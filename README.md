## Overview

The **Simple Interest Calculator** helps individuals and financial planners compute the total interest earned or paid over a specified period. Unlike compound interest, simple interest is calculated solely on the initial principal amount.

## Formula Used

The calculator uses the standard simple interest formula:

$$A = P \cdot (1 + r \cdot t)$$

Where:
- **$I$** = Interest amount ($I = P \cdot r \cdot t$)
- **$P$** = Principal amount (initial sum of money)
- **$r$** = Annual interest rate (as a decimal, e.g., $5\% = 0.05$)
- **$t$** = Time period (in years)
- **$A$** = Total amount accumulated (Principal + Interest)
